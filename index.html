Game Guide

🎯 1. Primary Objective

Be the first player to guide your pawn across the 9×9 arena to any square on your opponent's baseline (or claim the golden center trophy in 3-Player mode).

[ Goal Baseline (Opposite Row) ] 🏁
↑ ↑ ↑ (Forward Sprint) ↑ ↑ ↑
[ Home Baseline (Bottom Row) ] 🔵 (Your Pawn)

♟️ 2. Movement Rules

On your turn, advance your pawn one square orthogonally (forward, backward, left, right), provided no wall blocks the passage.

• Jumping Opponents: If rival pawns are directly adjacent with no wall behind them, jump over them into the square directly behind.

[ Empty Target Square ]
↑ (Leap)
[ 🔴 Rival Pawn ]
[ 🔵 Your Pawn ]

🧱 3. Wall Barrier Placement

Instead of advancing, deploy a 2-tile length laser wall (Horizontal [—] or Vertical [ | ]) to detour opponents.

• Fair Path Rule: You cannot completely enclose any player. At least one open path to the goal baseline must remain available for all players.

⛔ Full cage entrapment = Illegal move
✅ Long labyrinth detour = Tactical & Legal!
